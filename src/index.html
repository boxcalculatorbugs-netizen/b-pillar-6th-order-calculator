    Overview

    This calculator designs a 6th-order bandpass sub wall (typically behind the B-pillar). You enter vehicle space, driver info, chamber tuning, and port geometry. The app computes port lengths, gross volumes, packaging fit, estimated in-car response, and warnings.

    Number fields have − / + buttons to step values, or click the field to select and type a new number directly.

    All response and T/S outputs are estimates — verify critical builds in WinISD or Hornresp before cutting wood.

    File / Edit / Help

    • Save Design — writes every input and unit setting to a .json file.
    • Load Design — restores a saved .json design file.
    • Auto-save — inputs are saved to browser storage automatically; refresh restores your last session.
    • Load Design — restores a saved file.
    • Clear Design — clears all numeric inputs, clears optional T/S fields, unchecks toggles, and resets units to cu ft / in / sq in.
    • Port length model (Edit menu) — choose Helmholtz (default), Quarter Wave, or All Considered Theory (experimental MLTL). Affects calculated port length in area mode and default length in diameter/slot modes.
    • Color theme (Edit menu) — Midnight, Carbon, Ocean, Forest, or Slate Light. Saved automatically for next launch.
    • Door tuning (Helmholtz) (Edit → Experimental) — treats the open door as a short port on cabin volume. Enter door mouth width × height, jamb thickness, and optional cabin seal/leakage. Warns when door resonance is within ±5 Hz of front/external tuning.
    • End correction — follows port input: Round / Aero for area or diameter (auto 0.732×r or 0.614×r); Rectangular Slot for slot input (common walls 0–3, k 1.346–2.050×r).

    Order types

    ParallelBoth chambers port directly into the cabin. End correction is set per chamber via port style: Round / Aero uses 0.732×r on both ports; Rectangular Slot uses your common-walls setting.
    SeriesRear chamber ports into the front chamber (internal port). Front chamber ports to cabin (external). Only the external port receives cabin boost. Round / Aero: internal 0.614×r, external 0.732×r. Rectangular Slot: set common walls per port in each chamber section.
    PortedSingle-chamber vented box — one net volume and one port to the cabin. Chamber 2 inputs are hidden. End correction follows port input (Round/Aero or Slot).
    4th-Order BandpassSealed rear (Vr) + ported front (Vf, F2) to cabin. Fcb = Fs × √(1 + Vas/Vr); Qtc = Qts × √(1 + Vas/Vr). Warns when Fcb > F2. Low Fcb / low extension is sub-dependent: Fcb cannot go below Fs — enlarging Vr only approaches Fs. Shop low Fs, low Cms (stiff suspension → lower Vas), and Qts ~0.29–0.30, then size Vr so Fcb ≤ F2. Vf/Vr profiles: <1.5 wide, 1.5–2.5 street banger, ≥2.5 SPL burp.

    Tuning & frequency metrics

    Fb1 / Fb2Target tuning (Hz) in area mode. In diameter or slot mode, Fb is computed from port geometry and locked — adjust W, H, L instead.
    Est. bandwidthUsable range Fb1×0.85 to Fb2×1.15 shown as a frequency span (not Hz spread). Tuning spread = log₂(Fb2/Fb1). Volume ratio = front ÷ rear net volume.
    Cabin boost @ Fb2Estimated dB gain at the front tuning frequency from cabin loading. Doors closed: 12 dB/oct below onset. Doors open: 3 dB/oct (broken vessel / boundary loading only).
    Tolerance sweepWhen enabled, models ±% chamber volume error and shows how Fb drifts or port length must change to hold tuning.

    Volume metrics

    Net volumeAir volume inside the chamber after subtracting port, driver, baffle, and obstructions. Used directly in the port-length formula.
    Net vol / subChamber net volume divided by total driver count — airspace per woofer for that chamber. Also shown under the net volume input while in net basis.
    Gross volumeTotal constructed chamber volume including displacements.
    Volume basis — NetYou enter target net cu ft (required airspace). The breakdown adds port, driver, baffle, and other displacements to show built gross volume needed.
    Volume basis — Gross L×W×HYou enter box dimensions. App subtracts wall loss (if outer dims), port, driver share, baffle, and extra displacement to get effective net.
    Volume basis — Gross volumeSingle gross number; net = gross minus all displacements.
    Wall panel lossVolume consumed by doubled walls when measuring outer dimensions (2 × wall thickness per axis).
    Baffle panelHalf of the internal divider displacement (largest chamber gross W × H × baffle thickness when gross dims are entered) subtracted per chamber in dual-chamber designs.
    Bracing allowanceExtra % of chamber volume reserved for internal braces and structure (default 15%). Turn off if already included in gross measurements.
    Port displacementAir volume the port occupies inside the chamber (area × length). Always included in gross/net breakdown. Series 6th: use the rear-chamber slider to assign internal port volume to the front chamber (0% = all rear, 100% = all front).
    Extra displacementFixed cu in subtracted per chamber for non-port obstructions (amp niches, etc.).
    Port displacementInternal volume occupied by the port tunnel (area × length).
    Total net / grossSum of both chambers for cabin coupling checks.
    Effective cabin volumeTotal vehicle interior − built gross box − amp rack displacement. Used for box/cabin ratio and door tuning.
    Box / effective cabin ratioTotal box net volume ÷ effective cabin volume. Very low or high ratios may affect cabin loading (see warnings).

    Port metrics

    Port areaCross-section of the port opening (sq in). Larger area = shorter port for same Fb, but needs more wall space.
    Port area / net volPort cross-section divided by chamber net volume (sq in per cu ft). Common slot-port sizing ratio shown per chamber.
    Slot (W × H)Outer slot dimensions plus port wall thickness (default 0.75 in per chamber in slot mode if blank). Inner opening = (W − 2×wall) × (H − 2×wall) — shown live when slot mode is selected.
    Port lengthArea mode: calculated from the selected port model (Edit → Port length model). Diameter & slot modes: enter built port length, or leave blank to use calculated value. Slot uses W × H × L.
    HelmholtzClassic chamber + port resonance: length from Fb, net volume, and port area with end correction.
    Quarter Waveλ/4 tube length (c ÷ 4Fb) with end correction — volume-independent.
    All Considered (MLTL)Experimental pipe-cavity blend: L = (c/2πf) × arctan((A×c)/(2πf×V)). Blends Helmholtz and quarter-wave behavior by chamber size.
    Round / Aero PortPer-chamber port style. Auto end correction: 0.732×r (external/flanged) or 0.614×r (series internal). Best for round and aero ports.
    Rectangular Slot PortPer-chamber port style. Common walls (0–3): k scales from 1.346×r to 2.050×r. Common walls field appears when this style is selected.
    Port : Sd ratioPort area ÷ total system cone area. <0.5:1 severe compression risk. 0.5–0.75: acceptable. ≥0.75: excellent airflow.
    Port velocity @ XmaxEstimated air speed in the port at peak excursion. >25 m/s: caution. >35 m/s: chuffing likely — enlarge port.

    Driver & T/S metrics

    Sub size / countEstimates Sd (cone area) from industry lookup or 80% effective radius for custom sizes.
    SdEffective piston area (sq in). Used for port velocity, port:Sd ratio, and excursion estimates.
    FsFree-air resonance (Hz). Fb1 below ~0.7×Fs risks unloading.
    Qts / QesTotal / electrical Q. Lower Qts (<0.45) suits bandpass; Fs/Qes ≥45–50 preferred for 6th order.
    VasEquivalent compliance volume — scales suggested chamber sizes in T/S suggestions.
    XmaxMaximum linear excursion (mm) — drives port velocity and excursion warnings.
    VdPeak displacement volume (cu in). Optional; otherwise Sd × Xmax estimates driver displacement.
    Driver displacementVolume occupied by the motor/basket assembly inside the chamber.
    Suitability scoreHeuristic 0–100 based on Fs/Qes and Qts for 6th-order bandpass use.

    Cabin & in-car metrics

    Longest cabin dimensionDrives cabin onset frequency (typically floor-to-roof or longest interior axis).
    Include cabinWhen unchecked, cabin gain, coupling, and in-car overlays are omitted — useful for outdoor or trunk-only builds.
    Total vehicle interior volumePublished passenger/cargo interior volume from specs — starting point before subtracting box and amp rack.
    Amp rack volumeDisplacement of amplifiers, processors, and related gear in the cabin (cu ft).
    Effective cabin volumeVehicle interior − built gross box − amp rack. Used for box/cabin ratio and door Helmholtz when cabin is included.
    Door tuning (experimental)Door-jamb Helmholtz tuning: F_door from cabin volume, door mouth W×H, jamb thickness (+ end correction), and optional parasitic leak area when the cabin is not perfectly sealed. Chamber 3 shows jamb vs correction breakdown, leak/total vent area, closed cabin slope, and recommended jamb to match F2/Fb. Typical jamb 2–8 in; door mouth ~18–30 × 24–42 in. SPL door-open: align F_door to front tuning; musical: keep >5 Hz separation.
    Cabin sealed / leakageWhen door tuning is enabled: Perfectly sealed = 12 dB/oct closed-door cabin gain and zero parasitic vent. Unsealed cabins degrade closed slope by 0.2 dB/oct per sq in of estimated leak area (floor 3 dB/oct). Leak area adds to door mouth area for F_door when not sealed.
    Cabin Gain chartEstimated low-frequency boost below onset — solid = doors closed, dashed = doors open.
    Passband estimateSimplified dual-resonance curve from Fb1 and Fb2 (not a full T/S simulation).
    In-car overlayPassband + cabin boost combined for a rough in-vehicle level estimate.

    Build adjustments

    Wall thicknessDefault 1.5 in (2× 3/4" MDF). Subtracted from outer L×W×H on each axis when "measure from outer dims" is checked.
    Baffle thicknessInternal divider between chambers (default 0.75 in). Each chamber loses half of (largest gross W × H × baffle thickness) when gross L×W×H is entered. Zero baffle displacement in net-only mode. Not applied for single-chamber ported designs.

    Charts & warnings

    Box Diagram — cross-section with chamber volumes, port routing (series vs parallel), cabin as Ch. 3 when door tuning is enabled, and packaging notes.

    Cabin Gain — boost vs frequency with Fb1, Fb2, estimated usable edges, and cabin onset markers.

    Passband Estimate — relative passband shape plus in-car overlay (closed vs open) when cabin is included; passband only when cabin is excluded.

    Warnings aggregate port compression, velocity, packaging, T/S limits, excursion, and cabin coupling issues. Green = OK, amber = review, red = likely problem.
